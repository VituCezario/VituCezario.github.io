KPMG Brazil

Tax analysis, tax assessment and data organization: invoice review, building controls, and extracting large volumes of information.

It's also where my work with the Lei do Bem (Federal Law 11,196/2005) comes from — Brazil's R&D tax incentive, which lets a company deduct part of what it invests in Research, Development and Innovation. Except the benefit doesn't fall from the sky: someone has to prove, in technical language and with evidence, that there was genuine technological uncertainty — and not routine work. That's where I come in.
